===================================================================================
   FREIGHT MANAGEMENT SYSTEM (FMS) - SETTINGS IMPACT & DEPENDENCY TESTING GUIDE

This document provides a comprehensive mapping of all Settings views, their exact 
URLs, underlying database tables, downstream operational views affected, and step-
by-step testing workflows to assist the QA and development teams in validating 
system-wide dynamic behavior and settings cascade impact.

1. CURRENCY TABLE
===================================================================================
• Settings View URL:      http://localhost:8000/accounting/currency-table
• Print View URL:         http://localhost:8000/accounting/currency-table-print
• Database Tables:        currency_rates, accounting_block_dates
• Primary Controller:     App\Http\Controllers\CurrencyRateController

AFFECTED DOWNSTREAM VIEWS:
  1. Ocean Import Shipment Charges Tab
     - URL: http://localhost:8000/ocean-import/shipment
     - Impact: Auto-converts foreign currency charges (e.g., CAD/EUR) into local currency (USD) totals.
  2. Ocean Export Shipment & Vessel Schedule
     - URL: http://localhost:8000/ocean-export/booking & /ocean-export/vessel-schedule
     - Impact: Bulk currency conversions on MBL/HBL charges and freight schedules.
  3. Air Import & Air Export Shipments
     - URL: http://localhost:8000/air-import/shipment & /air-export/shipment
     - Impact: Master & House Air Waybill freight charge conversions.
  4. Truck Shipment Accounting Tab
     - URL: http://localhost:8000/truck/create/{id}
     - Impact: Multi-currency billing ledger conversion rates for cartage and drayage.
  5. Customer Invoicing & General Journal
     - URL: http://localhost:8000/accounting/invoice-create & /accounting/journal-entry
     - Impact: Calculates foreign exchange gain/loss and converts multi-currency invoice lines.

QA TESTING CHECKLIST:
  [ ] Test 1.1: Navigate to Currency Table. Add new rate CAD -> USD = 0.750000. Click "Save All".
  [ ] Test 1.2: Open Ocean Import Shipment charge tab. Add a CAD charge of $100.
  [ ] Test 1.3: Verify converted USD total displays as $75.00 dynamically.
  [ ] Test 1.4: Update rate in Currency Table to 0.800000 -> Verify shipment total updates to $80.00.
  [ ] Test 1.5: Test Excel Export and Print report -> Verify active search/currency filters are preserved.
